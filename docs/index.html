01 / THE RESEARCH IN BRIEF

Abstract

Reliable embodied execution requires more than generating a plausible plan: an embodied brain must understand physical causes of failure, adapt its guidance to the executor’s capabilities, and use interaction history to revise subsequent decisions. We introduce Embodied-Corrector, a corrective brain trained from RynnBrain-8B to connect observed outcomes with causal diagnosis and executable recovery.

We construct controlled failure–recovery branches from successful trajectories, changing one physical factor at a time. Evaluation-first SFT first teaches outcome judgment and cause analysis, then recovery planning. Noise-Aware Entropy-Gated Policy Optimization (NEPO) combines group-relative rewards with token-level guidance across generated and verified evaluation contexts. At deployment, the model produces Judge → Cause → Sub → Fine from public execution evidence and within-episode history.

The manuscript evaluates failure reasoning, planning transfer, and recovery with fixed execution backends. It reports 55.0% → 68.9% macro-average success with a fixed code backend and 1.2% → 7.3% success for a frozen π0.5 on RoboCasa365 Composite-Unseen. These simulation results connect improved diagnosis with more effective recovery while keeping the executor fixed.

02 / WHY RECOVERY MATTERS

Motivation

A capable brain must know what went wrong.

Physical execution is constrained by the embodiment, controller, and environment. How can an embodied brain explain unexpected outcomes and use feedback to plan a recovery its current executor can realize?

01 ◎

Understand the cause

Locate the action, relation, or missing prerequisite that first invalidated progress—not just the unsuccessful outcome.

Physical causal understanding
02 ⌘

Adapt to the executor

Translate a recovery into state-conditioned subtasks that the current controller or frozen VLA can actually realize.

Capability-aware planning
03 ↻

Learn from the interaction

Use prior actions, observations, and corrections as evidence for the next decision, without updating model parameters.

In-context adaptation

03 / HOW WE TRAIN THE CORRECTIVE BRAIN

Method

Assess first. Then plan a recovery.

We train RynnBrain-8B with evaluation-first SFT and NEPO. A single inference connects execution evidence to four structured outputs, giving the same downstream planner a better basis for its next action.

EVALUATION-FIRST TRAINING

Turn verified evaluations into the model’s own reasoning.

First learn Judge + Cause, then learn Sub + Fine conditioned on verified evaluations. NEPO combines group-relative rewards with entropy-gated on-policy distillation to reduce the training-to-deployment mismatch.

  • 1Learn to assess before learning to continue.
  • 2Retain positive guidance; attenuate uncertain negative signals.
  • 3Generate all four fields in one deployment pass.

Verified evaluation labels are available during training only.

INSIDE NEPO

Same policy. Same planning tokens. Two evaluation contexts.

Training can use a verified evaluation, while deployment must rely on the model’s own assessment. NEPO turns this context difference into a token-level learning signal.
